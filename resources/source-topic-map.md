## After-lab creative challenges (Try It Yourself)

The last step offers three optional, no-code extensions of Step 05: personalize the mini app, compare light and dark modes, and discover the results of interactive buttons. This is **new, instructor-designed practice**, not a reproduced passage from the textbook. Earlier suggested DartPad `print()` experiments are deferred to Session 03 because the instructor requested an engaging no-code alternative for the download wait.
